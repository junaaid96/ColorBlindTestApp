Everything in one app

Ishihara-style plates

A quick 7-plate screening or a full 16-plate test. Plates are made on the device from confusion-line colours, so you can't memorise them.

Result report

Shows type, severity and confidence, with a breakdown for each plate. Share it as text or PDF.

Hue arrangement (D-15 style)

Put 15 caps in order. It is scored with confusion index, selectivity, crossings and the likely axis.

Contrast sensitivity

An adaptive staircase on a faint directional arrow gives you a log CS score.

Color Lens

The live camera names the colour under the crosshair. It can simulate protan, deutan and tritan vision, or enhance colours in real time, and it can speak names aloud.

Photo simulator

Compare any photo side by side as seen with each type of colour vision deficiency. It has a severity slider and a daltonize toggle.

Daily practice

A 60-second odd-hue game with levels, lives, a best score and a daily streak.

4 languages and accessible

English, বাংলা, Español and हिन्दी. It uses a colour-blind-safe palette, TalkBack labels, a high-contrast theme and reduced motion. History trends are kept on your phone.

How it works

  1. Download and install the APK on any Android 8.0+ phone or tablet.
  2. Take a test. Start with the quick 7-plate screening, then try the full, hue and contrast tests.
  3. Read your report. It shows type, severity and confidence, and you can track trends in History.
  4. Explore. Point the Color Lens at the world or run photos through the simulator.

Installing the APK

  1. Tap Download APK on your Android device.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If you are asked, allow your browser or Files app to install unknown apps (Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps).
  3. Tap Install, then open Color Vision Lab.

About Play Protect

This is a debug build that isn't on the Play Store, so Google Play Protect may warn you or ask to scan it. You can choose Install anyway. The full source is public, and you can check the SHA-256 checksum above or build it yourself with ./gradlew assembleDebug.

Private by design

The app has no internet permission. It has no accounts, analytics or ads. Your results never leave your phone. The camera is used only for the Color Lens and processes frames on the device.

Screening, not a diagnosis

Color Vision Lab is a screening and learning tool. It is not a medical device. Phone screens vary in colour and brightness. If you have any concerns about your vision, see an eye-care professional.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. It is free and open source under the MIT license.

Which devices are supported?

Android 8.0 (API 26) and newer, including phone, tablet and foldable layouts.

Is it on the Play Store or iOS?

Not yet. For now it is a direct APK download from the GitHub releases page. It is Android only.

How accurate is it?

The plates use the Machado 2009 confusion-line model, with lightness noise so brightness gives no clue. Results still depend on your screen, so treat them as an indication.

Why does my phone warn me about the APK?

Android warns about any app installed from outside the Play Store. Debug builds also aren't signed with a release key. See the install section above.

How do I update?

Download the newer APK from this page and install it over the old one.

# Privacy

Color Vision Lab is fully offline.

- **No network access.** The app does not request the `INTERNET` permission.
- **No accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs.**
- **Camera** (optional) is used only by Color Lens. Frames are analysed in memory and never saved or transmitted.
- **Photos** chosen in the Simulator are opened through the system photo picker; the app gets read access to that single image only and does not copy it.
- **Results** (test history, streak, settings) are stored in app-private DataStore on the device and are excluded from cloud backup. Clear them any time in History → Clear history, or by uninstalling.
- **Sharing** a result or PDF happens only when you tap Share; you pick the destination.
